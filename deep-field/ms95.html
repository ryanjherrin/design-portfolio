Microsoft 1995 Rebuilt: Concept

I kept the Windows 95 desktop metaphor honest and rebuilt it for today.

Role Designer & Developer
Year TBD
Discipline UI Concept · Design Systems · Tokens
Hero shot: Microsoft 1995 Rebuilt

A love letter to the OS that started it all. It keeps the iconic desktop metaphor while introducing contemporary typography, spacing, and interaction patterns.

Not a skin, and not a parody. It's an exercise in what Win95 would look like if it were designed today, by someone who still believes in the spatial model.
