About

Ryan Herrin is an AI-focused Product Designer with over a year of experience building AI products. Currently Head of Product Design at Belva, where he built LawGoat — an AI-powered legal platform.

Specializing in complex systems, design systems, and AI-native interfaces. From component architecture in Figma to shipping production-ready designs, every decision is rooted in clarity and craft.

Open to product design roles focused on AI, developer tools, and complex software.
